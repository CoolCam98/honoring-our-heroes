2026 Keynote Speaker

Judge Michael Scionti.

We're pleased to announce that Judge Michael Scionti will serve as the Keynote Speaker for our 8th Annual Honoring Our Heroes event on November 1, 2026.

Judge Scionti is a U.S. military veteran who deployed multiple times to Iraq and Afghanistan. For the past ten years, he has led Hillsborough County's Veterans Treatment Court, helping justice-involved veterans rebuild their lives through accountability, treatment, mentorship, and recovery.

Veterans Treatment Courts provide eligible veterans with an alternative to incarceration by addressing underlying mental health and substance use challenges connected to military service. The program brings together judges, attorneys, VA specialists, community organizations, and veteran peer mentors to support long-term recovery and successful reintegration.

Representatives from our organization recently attended a Veterans Treatment Court graduation ceremony, where several veterans successfully completed the program. Witnessing the graduates alongside the volunteer veteran mentors who guide them through recovery was a powerful reminder of the importance of service beyond the uniform.

We are honored to welcome Judge Scionti as our 2026 keynote speaker and look forward to hearing his perspective on leadership, service, recovery, and the veteran community.
