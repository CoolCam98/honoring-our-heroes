Support Us · Become a Sponsor

Pick a sponsor level.

Your generous sponsorship covers the costs of the Honoring Our Heroes luncheon — the venue, food, sound system, event insurance, advertising, and door prizes. In-kind donations are welcome too.

Honoring Our Heroes is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so sponsorships are tax-deductible. We accept cash or check only — make checks payable to Honoring Our Heroes with "Veterans Event" on the memo line.

Where it goes

Every dollar goes to the day.

Honoring Our Heroes is run by volunteers with no overhead. Sponsorships pay for the luncheon itself, so veterans and a guest can attend for free.
