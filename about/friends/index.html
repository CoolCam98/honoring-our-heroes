About · Friends of HOH

Friends of HOH.

Causes we're proud to stand beside in service of those who served. Each one supports veterans in a different way — and each welcomes your help.

Organizations we support

  • Handmade quilts for veterans

    Quilts of Valor

    Covering Service Members and Veterans touched by war with comforting, healing quilts. One Nation. One Mission. Each year 4–5 deserving Veterans receive a customized quilt handmade by the local QOV Tangled Threads team, led by Master Sgt. (Ret.) Tammy Arnold.

    Visit Quilts of Valor

  • Care for veterans' dogs

    Chad's Promise

    A free safe haven for dogs while their veteran owners are in the hospital. Chad provides emergency pickup for veterans' dogs while they receive medical or mental-health treatment. Chad Landry and his partner, Tracy Swinscoe, also run a dog-training business in Plant City.

    7314 W Knights Griffin Rd, Plant City, FL
    (813) 296-4906 · contact@chadspromise.com

    Boarding & Donations

  • Remembering the fallen

    Wreaths Across America

    Over 1,700 veterans rest in Plant City's cemeteries. For the past four years, HOH has helped place wreaths on their graves — beginning with a ceremony at Veterans Park, then on to the Plant City Cemetery Office for assignments. National Wreaths Across America Day is Saturday, December 19, 2026.

    Questions? Kimberly Kitchens, Area Coordinator, Plant City · (813) 562-4495

    Visit Wreaths Across America

  • Trips to Washington, DC

    Honor Flight Network

    Transporting American Veterans to Washington, DC to honor and celebrate them — first WWII veterans, now Korean War, Vietnam War, and critically ill veterans through the Honor Flight TLC program. As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, donations are tax-deductible.
